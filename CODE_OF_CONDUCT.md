# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Serverless Creed to be a welcoming place to learn and to teach, for people of every background, experience level and identity. Beginners asking basic questions are exactly who this project is for.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially in reviews. Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully, technical or otherwise.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the people reading it later.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **vidit@serverlesscreed.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Depending on severity, the first step is usually a private warning.
